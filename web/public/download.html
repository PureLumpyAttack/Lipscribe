Lipscribe for your computer

The desktop app reads your lips on your own computer and speaks for you, in a natural voice or your own, straight into Zoom, Google Meet, Teams or Discord as if it were your microphone.

Download for macOS (Apple silicon) Download for macOS (Intel) Download for Windows

Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu → About This Mac: "Apple M…" is Apple silicon, "Intel" is Intel. All versions are on the releases page.

The model is built in (about 270 MB download), so lip reading works offline and the video never leaves your computer. Natural and cloned voices use the Lipscribe voice service.

Use it as a microphone in calls

  1. Install a virtual audio cable (free): BlackHole 2ch on macOS, VB-CABLE on Windows. It adds a pretend speaker and microphone that are wired together.
  2. In Lipscribe, pick a natural voice or set up your own voice, then set Play through to the cable (BlackHole 2ch or CABLE Input). Keep Also play on this computer on to hear yourself.
  3. In your call app, choose the cable as the microphone (BlackHole 2ch or CABLE Output).
  4. Press ⌘⇧L (macOS) or Ctrl+Shift+L (Windows) to start and stop reading your lips, even while the call app is in front. Turn on View → Keep on top to keep the camera view visible.

The same works in the browser demo in Chrome or Edge, as long as the tab stays open.

First launch

The app isn't signed yet. On macOS, right-click Lipscribe in Applications and choose Open the first time. On Windows, choose More info → Run anyway. Allow camera access when asked.
