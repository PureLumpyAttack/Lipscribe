Voice

Speak into Zoom, Meet or Discord
  1. Install a free virtual audio cable: BlackHole 2ch on macOS, VB-CABLE on Windows.
  2. Pick a natural voice or your own voice above, then set Play through to the cable (BlackHole 2ch / CABLE Input).
  3. In the call app, choose the cable as your microphone (BlackHole 2ch / CABLE Output).

Works in Chrome and Edge. The desktop app does the same without a browser tab and adds a global shortcut.
