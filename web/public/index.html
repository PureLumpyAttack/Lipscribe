Say it without sound

Lipscribe reads your lips through a webcam, then writes and speaks the words for you. For loud rooms, silent ones, and anyone who can't use their voice.

Loud rooms

Cafés, platforms, concerts. Mouth the words and Lipscribe types and says them, no shouting needed.

“Could I get some water, please?”

Without a voice

After throat surgery, with ALS, or on vocal rest. If your lips can move, you can still be understood.

“The baby just fell asleep.”

Quiet places

Libraries, hospital wards, late nights. Talk to someone across the room without making a sound.

How it works

No microphone, no app to install. A webcam and a browser are enough.

  1. 1

    Face the camera

    Open the demo and allow your webcam. Lipscribe finds your mouth and greys out everything else.

  2. 2

    Mouth the words

    Press Record and speak silently. Only the video is used, so noise around you doesn't matter.

  3. 3

    Read it, hear it

    The words appear as text and are read aloud in the voice you pick. Every line stays in your history.

Q&A

What is Lipscribe?

A web app that turns lip movements into text and speech. It works from video of your mouth, so it needs no sound at all.

Does it use my microphone?

No. Lipscribe only asks for the camera and only records video. Whether you speak out loud or silently makes no difference.

What do I need to try it?

A laptop or phone with a camera and a recent Chrome, Edge or Safari. Even light on your face helps the most.

Where does my video go?

The live camera view stays in your browser. A clip is sent to the Lipscribe model only after you press Stop, and only that clip.

How accurate is it?

The model we build on, Auto-AVSR, gets about four out of five words right on the LRS3 benchmark. Short, clear phrases facing the camera work best.

Who made it?

A small team at a hackathon in November 2026. The code is open on GitHub.
